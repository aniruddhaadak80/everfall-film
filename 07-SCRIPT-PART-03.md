# EVERFALL — PART 03: "THE IRON BOY"
## Full script (simple English)

**Runtime target:** 4–5 minutes | **Shots:** 19 | **Format:** 1080p, sound + voices included

---

### SCENE 1 — THE TRAP (no dialogue, 30 seconds)

*Night. An old industrial district. A tram has crashed against a building. Children are trapped.*

*AARAV runs toward it. He does not see the cameras. He does not see the foundry walls.*

*The tram flickers — a hologram. It dies. Aarav stops. The walls begin to close.*

---

### SCENE 2 — THE CELL (Aarav + Vane, 90 seconds)

*Aarav wakes in a glass cell. Blue rings dim. He cannot feel his fingers.*

*VANE visits. Polite. Interested. Like a teacher.*

**VANE:**
> "Iron is the one thing old magic cannot control. Did you know that?"
> "Your shard grew around iron. That makes you… special. That makes you the key."

**AARAV:**
> "I'm not a key. I'm a person."

**VANE (warm):**
> "Of course you are. And people can be keys. I'm not cruel, Aarav. I'm precise."

---

### SCENE 3 — THE PRICE (Vane, 60 seconds)

*Vane touches Aarav's temple. A red thread of light passes into his palm.*

*Aarav's face goes slack. The blue rings on his arm go dark.*

**AARAV (whisper, after):**
> "…What did you take?"

**VANE:**
> "A face. A father's face. I keep them like books."
> "When this is over, I'll give it back. I promise."

*He leaves. Aarav cannot remember his father's face anymore.*

---

### SCENE 4 — THE BETRAYAL (Aiko, 80 seconds)

*Aiko's tower lab. The screens show Aarav's shard readings. A flatline prediction. TERMINAL.*

*She knows: after the sky opens, Vane will not need Aarav anymore.*

*She swipes the stolen keycard. Runs. Slams the red switch.*

*Every light in the machine hall dies. The glass cells hiss open.*

**AIKO (into her radio):**
> "…I don't know how to be good yet. But I know how to open doors."

---

### SCENE 5 — THE CALL (all, 40 seconds)

*Six points of blue light pulse across the city. All six feel it at once.*

*BO grabs his glasses. ROSE clutches her chest. LENA freezes mid-step.
AIKO watches the city from the tower. GHOST turns toward the tower.*

**GHOST (two voices):**
> "He's at the tower. He's coming back."

---

### SCENE 6 — THE COMPASS (Lena, 50 seconds)

*Lena holds the dead shard. A thin blue line points to the tower.*

**LENA:**
> "The dead thing knows where the living thing is. Fine."
> "We go tonight. Bring the nun."

---

### SCENE 7 — THE DOOR (all, 80 seconds)

*The six stand before the tower's service gate. Aiko meets them with the access card.*

**AIKO:**
> "I got you in. One problem."

*She shows them the hologram: the red shard, stitched into Vane's glove lining.*

**AIKO:**
> "The glove is the connection. Cut the glove, cut the signal."
> "And when you see him… he will be kind. He is always kind. That is the trap."

---

### SCENE 8 — THE IRON BOY (all, 30 seconds)

*Aarav looks at his own hand. The blue rings are back — brighter than before.*

**AARAV:**
> "He took my father's face. I want it back."
> "And then I'm going to close the sky."

*The six walk through the door. The tower hums.*

*Title card: **EVERFALL — THE IRON BOY***

**— END OF PART 03 —**
