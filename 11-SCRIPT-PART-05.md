# EVERFALL — PART 05: SIX BRUISES

> Part 03 `The Iron Boy` · Part 04 `Shadow Detective` · **Part 05 `Six Bruises`** · next: Part 06 `Beneath the City`
>
> Logline: To rob the sky, the team rehearses its own losses — and pays them for real. Bo's scream goes silent forever, and the last stranger in the team finally gets a name.

## THE RULES OF THIS PART
- **Prices are not metaphors.** They are receipts the body signs in real time.
- **Extraction theory (Aiko):** things the sky keeps are *stored, not spent*. If a jump binds two shadows, one thing can be pulled back. Proof will be needed. Proof costs.
- **Ghost's time-skip** skips 3 seconds forward for everyone else. The trade-in is one day of Ghost's memory. Nobody on the team has totaled what Ghost still has left.

## CAST IN THIS PART
LENA — leader now, by vote nobody mentions.
AARAV — back in army-cold shape, shorting heat lamps.
BO — 13, loud, scared of doctors, and the soul of the team.
ROSE — 58, warrior nun, already spent four years of life on-screen. Knows the arithmetic.
AIKO — the brain, gravity-bent, wrists cracking.
GHOST — quiet. Half-erased. About to be found.
VANE — off-screen this part: felt everywhere, seen nowhere. His drones circle the safehouse like patient gulls.

## SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — INT. SAFEHOUSE — PLANNING BOARD — DAY
A wall of receipts: photos, tags, torn notebook pages. father-face. mara. hearing. years. memory. The team's debts, written in chalk.

> **LENA** — *He kept every price on a shelf. Fine. Before we open the sky — we learn to reach through it. One pull. One proof.*
> **AIKO** — *Theory: two bound shadows make a door. Through a door, one item comes back. Small. Dry. Unaged.*

### SCENE 2 — EXT. YARD — REHEARSAL — GOLDEN HOUR
Each hero pays a little, on purpose, for practice:
- **ROSE** — drops her light-blade mid-swing and visibly ages one week after a single use. She marks it in a small black book: tally marks.
- **AIKO** — catches a falling tram car and lands with a wrist bent wrong, bones heavy as sandbags.
- **AARAV** — pulls iron filings off a wall; frost stairs from his breath like engine exhaust.
- **LENA** — two jumps; loses two minutes; the team notices her asking "what day is it."

### SCENE 3 — INT. STAIRWELL PAST — THE PROOF RUN
An echo-stairwell. Lena binds her shadow to Bo's below. Aiko holds the door with a gravity-well. Lena reaches in and pulls out a *shoe* — dust dry, forty years light, size of a nine-year-old.

> **LENA** — *Proof. He keeps everything. Which means everything can be stolen back.*
> **BO** — *That's Mara's shoe. That means Mara's IN there. That means rescue Saturday.*
> **GHOST** — *Careful. Doors that open one way… open the other way too.*

### SCENE 4 — EXT. SAFEHOUSE — COLLAPSE — NIGHT
A drone strike hits the west wall. Beams come down between Aarav and the team. Ghost steps into the falling dark, skips three seconds — everyone else snaps forward, Ghost is already through — and shoves Aarav clear.
Then: *price*. Ghost blinks, looks around like a tourist.

> **GHOST** — *…Who are you? Where is the bookshop? Was there a wall?*
> **AIKO** *(quiet, appalled)* — *He's lost another day. He doesn't remember the plan. He doesn't remember us.*

### SCENE 5 — EXT. YARD FIGHT — THE LAST SCREAM
Drones pour in. Bo takes the roof line and does his job: SCREAM. The shockwave drops four drones in a hail. Mid-scream, the sound cuts out — not outside. Inside Bo. The world goes asleep-quiet. The shard on his chest flickers and goes DARK. He keeps screaming and hears nothing, feels nothing, and the air doesn't move.

> **BO** *(screaming, unheard)* — *WHY IS IT QUIET. WHY CAN'T YOU HEAR ME? WHY CAN'T I—*
> **ROSE** — *Bo. Bo. Enough. Enough, little thunder. You're done. Done.*

Rose ends the fight alone — one light-blade, one year of her life — tally mark. The drones walk home empty.

### SCENE 6 — INT. SAFEHOUSE — HOSPITAL CORNER — LATER
Bo sits with cotton in his ears, glasses taped, furious tears. He puts his palm on the floor and can feel Rose's footsteps — vibration, not sound.

> **BO** *(signs; Lena reads aloud)* — *I can still see it. In the floor. Everything loud makes circles.*
> **AARAV** — *You're the only one of us whose power was kindness that got loud. It doesn't leave. It just learned a new door.*

Bo laughs — a real laugh — and the team learns to knock on tables instead of shouting.

### SCENE 7 — INT. THE ARCHIVIST'S BOOKSHOP — NIGHT (THE REVEAL)
Ghost stands in the doorway, holding the paper bag of nothing they took in. The Archivist looks up, pours a third cup of tea, and puts it where Ghost was — not where Ghost is.

> **ARCHIVIST** — *You came in with the light rain. Forty years ago. Child's ticket. I still have your book — "100 lugares antes de morir." You paid with the only day you had.*
> **GHOST** — *I have no name.*
> **ARCHIVIST** — *You have mine. Sit, Tobi. This tea is garbage and you always drank it.*

Ghost sits. A name. A taste. Two days that are his.

> **ARCHIVIST** — *You are not a ghost, child. You are what the sky threw away empty. The first one it took and gave back when it finished looking at you.*

### SCENE 8 — EXT. SAFEHOUSE ROOF — DAWN (CLOSE PART 06'S DOOR)
The team chalks one new word on the receipts wall, crossing nothing out: **tobi**. Bo taps out a rhythm on the rail; Ghost taps back the same rhythm from memory. Somewhere below, the sewers breathe warm air — and beneath the city, something that was sleeping rolls over.

> **LENA (V.O.)** — *Six bruises, one each. We're not alive because we're strong. We're alive because we stopped charging interest.*

FADE OUT — Part 06: **BENEATH THE CITY**.
