## The exact prompts

### SHOT 01 — The fake emergency
```
Night street in an old industrial district, a tram has crashed against a building and small figures
of children are trapped inside, a 24-year-old Indian man in a grey hoodie runs toward the crash
from an alley, emergency lights painting the wet asphalt red and blue, photorealistic, fast handheld
camera, cinematic tension, film grain
```

### SHOT 02 — The foundry
```
Interior of an abandoned iron foundry, massive cold furnaces and hanging chains, the crashed tram
is revealed to be a hologram projection that flickers and dies, a 24-year-old man in a grey hoodie
stops running and looks up at the walls closing in, photorealistic, slow dolly-in, cold industrial
light, film grain
```

```
A 24-year-old Indian man in a grey hoodie wakes sitting against the wall of a glass cell, his blue

and it rings like a bell, photorealistic, slow push-in, cold blue light, film grain
```

### SHOT 04 — Vane visits
```
A 62-year-old wealthy man in a black three-piece suit and black leather gloves stands outside a
glass cell, hands clasped behind his back, watching a young man inside with calm academic interest,

push-in, warm polite menace
```

### SHOT 05 — Iron is the key
```
Close-up through glass of an old man's gloved hand tracing the outline of a young man's face, a
```
