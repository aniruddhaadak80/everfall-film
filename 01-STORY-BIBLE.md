# EVERFALL — Story Bible

*Written in simple English so the whole team (and the AI) can follow.*

---

## 1. The world in one line

**Our world is a lid. Tonight, the lid cracks.**

## 2. What happened (history)

- 3,000 years ago, a star-city called **Everfall** fell from the sky and crashed into the earth.
- Six gods died in that fall. Their power soaked into the ground.
- People built a real city on top of the buried star-city. They forgot it was there.
- The magic slept. Until **The Crack** — the night the sky broke open like glass over the city.

## 3. The present (when our story starts)

Modern city with trams, old bridges, glass towers, and crowded streets — a mix of
old soul and new speed (think Kolkata, but bigger and stranger).

On the night of The Crack:
- The sky splits in a straight line. Blue fire falls like rain but does not burn.
- Some people touch the fire and **change**. They get power. Their eyes glow.
- Machines go crazy. Phones show dead languages. The dead stars fall into the city,
  and where they land, the old magic wakes up.

## 4. The rules of magic (important — keeps the movie honest)

1. **Power comes from a star-shard.** Each hero has one inside them. Hurt the shard, hurt the hero.
2. **Power has a price.** Every use costs something personal (memory, warmth, time, sound).
3. **Magic is visible.** It always looks like blue-white light inside the body, gold sparks when used.
4. **Old magic fears iron.** That is why Aarav's power is special — iron is the one thing old magic cannot control.
5. **You cannot fly.** Heroes jump, fall, get hurt. This keeps fights real and heavy.

## 5. The villain's want

**Malachi Vane** was a child during The Crack 40 years ago (yes — it happened once before and was covered up).
He did not get power then. He spent his life studying the star-shards.
He wants to open the sky **fully** and let Everfall rise — because he believes the old gods will make him one of them.
He is not crazy. He is patient. He is kind. That is what makes him scary.

## 7. Themes (what the movie is really about)

- **Belonging:** six strangers with nothing in common become a family
- **Price of power:** every gift takes something
- **Old and new:** the city's soul vs its speed
- **Forgiveness:** even the villain gets a human moment

## 8. Tone and look references

- **Look:** *Avengers* (team scale) + *Spider-Verse* (color and heart) + real photography of Kolkata streets at night + *Dune* (magic feels heavy, not cute)
- **Sound:** Indian percussion (tabla, dhol) under a big orchestra. Magic = strings + synth.
- **Rule:** the city must feel REAL. Real dust, real rain, real crowds. Magic is the only unreal thing.

## 9. Look consistency rules for the AI (very important)

- Every character keeps the SAME face in every shot. We use a reference image per character
  (a "look sheet") for all video generation. See 02-CHARACTERS.md.
- The city skyline is always: river + old bridge on the left, glass towers on the right.
- Magic color code is fixed: **cold blue** inside the body, **gold sparks** when used, **red** only for Vane.
