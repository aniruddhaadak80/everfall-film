# EVERFALL — The Characters

*Each character has: who they are, how they look (super detailed, so the AI can draw the same face every time),
how they sound, and their costume. The "LOOK SHEET PROMPT" line is what you paste into the image AI.*

---

## 1. AARAV MEHTA — "The Iron Boy" (main hero)

**Age 24. Works in his father's small repair shop. Fixes trams and fans. Tired eyes, kind hands.**

**How he looks:**
- Indian man, 24, short black hair, always a little messy
- Warm brown skin, thin tired face, a small scar on his left eyebrow
- Soft eyes — the kind that look away when they are sad
- Thin but strong working hands, oil under the nails
- 5'9", slim build, stands like someone who carries things for others

**Costume (after the Crack):**
- Old grey repair-shop hoodie with a torn sleeve
- Blue rings of light that sit on his skin like a tattoo when he uses power
- One glove on the left hand — the hand that pulls iron
- Simple trainers. No armor. He is not a soldier.

**Voice:** low, soft, speaks slow. Sounds like a person who thinks before talking.
**Power:** pulls iron from anything — buildings, roads, trams. He can lift a bus with his mind.
**Price:** every use makes his body colder. Too much use and he cannot feel his fingers.

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT (image AI):**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 24-year-old Indian man, short messy black hair, warm brown skin,
> thin tired face, small scar on left eyebrow, soft kind eyes, wearing a grey torn hoodie,
> blue glowing ring tattoos on his neck and left hand, cinematic studio lighting,
> looking straight at camera, ultra detailed skin texture, 85mm lens, movie poster quality

## 2. LENA KOVAC — "The Shadow Detective" (co-lead)

**Age 31. City police detective. Does not believe in magic. Until tonight.**

**How she looks:**
- White woman, 31, sharp cheekbones, short dark hair pushed behind her ears
- Grey-green eyes that look like they have not slept in days
- Thin lips, usually pressed tight. A hard jaw.
- 5'7", athletic, moves like a fighter who never starts a fight

**Costume:** old brown leather jacket, dark jeans, detective badge on a chain,
black boots. Practical. Nothing shiny.

**Voice:** flat, dry, quiet. Says cruel things in a calm voice. Funny without trying.
**Power:** steps through shadows. One shadow to another. Only if she can see both.
**Price:** each jump takes 1 minute of her memory. She is already forgetting her sister's face.

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT:**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 31-year-old woman detective, East European features,
> sharp cheekbones, short dark hair behind ears, grey-green tired eyes, wearing a
> worn brown leather jacket over a dark turtleneck, cinematic noir lighting,
> looking straight at camera, ultra detailed, 85mm lens, movie quality

## 3. BO — "The Loud Boy" (heart of the team)

**Age 13. Lives on the street. Sells phone chargers. Talks too much when scared.**

**How he looks:**
- Bengali boy, 13, thin as a stick, big round glasses taped at the corner
- Curly black hair, always dusty
- Two front teeth missing. Laughs with his whole body.
- Short for his age, fast, always slightly leaning forward like he is ready to run

**Costume:** oversized yellow rain jacket (found in trash, his favorite thing),
shorts, torn sandals, a bag of chargers on his back.

**Voice:** fast, high, funny. Says the thing everyone is thinking.
**Power:** sound. His scream breaks glass and stone. He can ride sound waves like a skateboard.
**Price:** he slowly goes deaf. By Part 05 he cannot hear his own name.

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT:**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 13-year-old Bangladeshi-Indian street boy, thin build,
> big round glasses taped at corner, curly dusty black hair, missing front tooth,
> huge happy grin, wearing an oversized yellow rain jacket, cinematic warm lighting,
> looking at camera, ultra detailed skin, 50mm lens, movie quality

## 4. SISTER ROSE — "The Light Blade" (the warrior)

**Age 58. Nun. Runs a shelter for lost children. Kind until she fights.**

**How she looks:**
- Indian woman, 58, silver hair in a tight bun, deep lines on her face
- Dark brown skin, strong shoulders from carrying children her whole life
- Eyes like a hawk — calm, no fear
- Tall and straight, stands like a soldier even in a saree

**Costume:** simple white cotton saree with a thin blue border. When she fights,
light comes out of the border like a blade of fire.

**Voice:** deep, warm, slow. Speaks like she is quoting something holy.
**Power:** makes light into a solid blade. Cuts anything. Only light.
**Price:** each fight takes one year of her life.

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT:**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 58-year-old Indian nun, silver hair in tight bun,
> dark brown skin, deep wise face lines, calm hawk-like eyes, wearing a simple white
> cotton saree with thin blue border, cinematic soft lighting, looking at camera,

## 5. DR. AIKO TANAKO — "The Gravity Woman" (the brain)

**Age 36. Physicist. Studies the star-shards. Works for Vane at first.**

**How she looks:**
- Japanese woman, 36, black hair in a sharp bob, red framed glasses
- Pale skin, thin nose, always a pen behind her ear
- 5'5", small, sits very still. Moves like a cat.
- A burn scar on her right wrist from a lab accident

**Costume:** white lab coat over a black shirt, black trousers, small gold earrings.
No superhero costume. She refuses to wear one.

**Voice:** precise, fast, no feelings in the voice. Then, slowly, feelings come back.
**Power:** gravity wells. Makes things float, makes things crush. Can hold a building in the air.
**Price:** her bones get heavier. In Part 08 she cannot walk without help.

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT:**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 36-year-old Japanese physicist woman, sharp black bob haircut,
> red framed glasses, pale skin, calm expression, small burn scar on right wrist,
> wearing a white lab coat over black shirt, cinematic cool lighting,
> looking at camera, ultra detailed, 85mm lens, movie quality

## 7. MALACHI VANE — "The Man Who Waited" (the villain — Part 01 to 07)

**Age 62. Rich. Philosophic. Kind to children. The most dangerous man in the city.**

**How he looks:**
- White man, 62, silver hair combed back, short grey beard
- Deep blue eyes, very calm — like a lake
- Tall, thin, perfect posture. Wears only hand-made suits.
- A small red mark under his left eye — where a star-shard touched him 40 years ago

**Costume:** black three-piece suit, silver pocket watch (an old star-shard in disguise),
black leather gloves. Never without the gloves.

**Voice:** soft, deep, warm. Like a good teacher. He never shouts. He never needs to.
**Power:** memory. He can take a memory out of your head and keep it.
**Price:** he has no memories of his own left. He keeps other people's like books.
**Face turn (Part 07):** he dies saving Bo. His last line: *"Finally... something that is mine."*

**LOOK SHEET PROMPT:**
> Photorealistic portrait of a 62-year-old wealthy man, silver hair combed back,
> short grey beard, deep calm blue eyes, small red mark under left eye,
> wearing a black three-piece suit with silver pocket watch, black leather gloves,
> cinematic dramatic lighting, looking at camera, ultra detailed, 85mm lens, movie quality
