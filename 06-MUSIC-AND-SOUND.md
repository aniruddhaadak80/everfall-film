# EVERFALL — Music & Sound Plan

*Simple English. Every part gets the same 6 themes. The audience learns them like friends.*

---

## The six themes (the "sound of the movie")

| # | Theme | Sounds like | Used when |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Everfall Main Theme** | Big orchestra + Indian dhol drums + a synth that sounds like the sky is crying | Title card, team scenes, ending |
| T2 | **Aarav's Theme** | Solo sitar with slow strings. Sad but warm. | Aarav alone, his father, quiet moments |
| T3 | **Vane's Theme** | Solo cello + a whisper under it. Very quiet. Very scary. | Vane on screen |
| T4 | **Battle Theme** | Fast tabla + electric guitar + brass. Heavy. | Fights |
| T5 | **Wonder Theme** | Church organ + children's choir. Big and open. | Magic, the Crack, the fly shots |
| T6 | **Price Theme** | One piano note that repeats, getting slower each time | When a hero pays for their power |

## Sound rules

1. **The Crack sound** is the signature of the movie: a long deep CRACK, then 1 second of total silence. Every part uses this exact silence.
2. **Blue fire** always sounds like glass chimes + a soft choir.
3. **Vane never gets loud music.** Scariest when quiet.
4. **The city is a character.** Always hear trams, river, crows, distant traffic under everything.

## Dialogue & voices

- The movie has **real voices, not narration robots.** Use the voice plan in PIPELINE.md (text-to-speech with the exact voice settings per character).
- Aarav speaks slow and low. Lena speaks flat. Bo speaks fast. Vane speaks soft and warm. Ghost has a slight double-echo.

## Where to get the music (options, you choose later)

1. **AI music** (Suno-type tools): generate each theme, then keep the same seed for every part → the movie sounds like one film.
2. **Stock/orchestra:** hire a composer with these 6 themes as the brief.
3. **Hybrid:** AI for themes T4–T6, real sitar/cello recordings for T2/T3.

*Rule: once a theme is recorded, every later part MUST reuse the same audio file. That is how a movie sounds like a movie.*
