# EVERFALL — PART 01: "THE CRACK"
## Full script (simple English)

**Runtime target:** 5–6 minutes | **Shots:** 16 | **Format:** 1080p, sound + voices included

---

### SCENE 1 — THE CITY AT NIGHT (no dialogue)

*The city sleeps. Wide shots. Trams, bridges, river, glass towers.*

**NARRATOR (V.O., old and warm — The Archivist):**
> "Before the sky broke, nobody looked up. We were all too busy looking down.
> That is how it happens. That is how it always happens."

*The river is dark. The bridge lights flicker. On the old bridge, a single tram sits still.*

---

### SCENE 2 — AARAV'S SHOP (no dialogue, 40 seconds)

*A small repair shop. Messy. A fan turns slowly. AARAV MEHTA, 24, fixes a radio by hand.*

*He is tired. He looks at a photo of his father on the wall — the shop used to be his father's.*

*On the radio, a news voice talks about a strange blue light seen over the sea. Aarav turns it off.*

**RADIO (V.O.):**
> "…scientists say it was probably a satellite. Nothing to worry about…"

*Aarav smiles a small, sad smile and goes back to the fan.*

---

### SCENE 3 — THE CRACK (no dialogue — the money shot)

*The sky splits. A straight line of white fire, like a glass plate breaking.*

*Silence for 1 second. Then the sound — a deep, long CRACK that shakes the whole city.*

*Blue fire starts falling like slow rain. It does not burn. It lands on roofs, roads, hands.*

*People come out of their houses and look up. Nobody moves.*

*On the old bridge, the tram's windows blow out. Aarav runs out of his shop and looks up.*

*One piece of blue fire falls toward him. It hits his chest —*

*— and everything goes white.*

---

### SCENE 4 — THE BRIDGE (no dialogue, 1 minute)

*Aarav wakes up on the road. The city is in chaos. The old bridge is cracking in half.*

*A piece of the bridge falls toward a small girl —*

*Aarav puts his hand out — and the iron in the bridge RIPS out and stops in the air.*

*Blue rings of light move up his arm. The iron slowly sets down, safe.*

*The girl looks at him. He looks at his own hand, terrified.*

**AARAV (whisper):**
> "What am I?"

*He runs.*

---

### SCENE 5 — THE CRIME SCENE (Lena, 50 seconds)

*A burned library. Books turned to blue glass. LENA KOVAC, detective, 31, walks in slowly.*

*A police officer runs to her.*

**OFFICER MISHRA:**
> "Detective. Every book in here turned to glass. No fire. No heat. Nothing."

*Aarav runs past, panicked. Lena catches his arm.*

**LENA:**
> "Hey. Hey! Look at me."

*Aarav pulls away — but his arm leaves a blue handprint on her wrist for one second.*

*Aarav runs off. Lena looks at her wrist. Then at the glass books.*

**LENA (quiet):**
> "…What are you?"

---

### SCENE 6 — THE OTHER FOUR (fast montage, 20 seconds — teaser)

*4 quick shots, no dialogue. Just power and price.*

1. **BO, 13,** on a rooftop, screams — the sound blows out every window on the street. He covers his ears, scared.
2. **SISTER ROSE, 58,** in her shelter, a broken table in front of her — light comes out of her saree like a blade and cuts it clean.
3. **DR. AIKO TANAKO, 36,** in a lab, a chair floats around her. She looks at her hands, and she is smiling — a small, wrong smile.
4. **GHOST** — a shadow on a wall with a gold ring in the eye. He looks straight at the camera. He knows we are watching.

---

### SCENE 7 — THE TOWER (Vane, 30 seconds)

*High above the city, a glass tower. MALACHI VANE, 62, stands at the window and watches the blue rain.*

*Behind him, a screen shows Aarav's face, and the bridge, and the blue handprint on Lena's wrist.*

*Vane is not angry. He is happy.*

**VANE (soft, warm):**
> "Six shards. All awake. Forty years of waiting. And the city gave them to me."

*He touches his silver pocket watch. Inside, a small star-shard glows red.*

**VANE:**
> "Thank you, Aarav Mehta."

*His eyes glow red for one second.*

---

### SCENE 8 — THE ARCHIVIST (20 seconds — closing)

*The old blind woman in the bookshop. She cannot see, but she looks up at the sky anyway.*

*She turns her head toward the door — like she can hear something coming.*

**ARCHIVIST:**
> "They are coming. Good. The gods are lonely."

*Title card: **EVERFALL** — white letters over the cracked sky.*

*Blue fire keeps falling. Cut to black.*

**— END OF PART 01 —**
