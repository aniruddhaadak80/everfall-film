# EVERFALL — PART 02: "WANTED SPARKS"
## Full script (simple English)

**Runtime target:** 4–5 minutes | **Shots:** 19 | **Format:** 1080p, sound + voices included

---

### SCENE 1 — MORNING AFTER (no dialogue, 30 seconds)

*The city wakes up afraid. News vans. Barricades. A giant screen shows six blurred photos and the word
TERRORISTS. Drones sweep the streets. People wear masks.*

*Under the city, in an abandoned metro station, AARAV sits alone in the dark. He looks at his own
hand. The blue rings are gone. He cannot feel his fingers anymore.*

---

### SCENE 2 — THE BOY (Bo, 40 seconds)

*A sound behind him. AARAV turns — the iron on the bench floats an inch.*

**AARAV:**
> "Who's there?"

*A 13-year-old boy steps out of the dark. Big glasses. Yellow rain jacket. Not afraid. Too afraid to show it.*

**BO:**
> "You're the bridge guy. I watched you. I watched you all night."
> "I've been watching you since the tram."

*Bo screams — a reflex. The sound hits the station and every tube light SHATTERS.*

*Aarav's hand flies up — and every shard of glass STOPS in the air.*

*They both stare at the floating glass.*

**BO (quiet):**
> "…Cool."

---

### SCENE 3 — THE SHELTER (Rose, 50 seconds)

*Rose's shelter. Police drones crash through the windows. Children scream.*

*Rose stands in front of them. The blue border of her saree rises — and becomes a blade of light.*

*She cuts the first drone in half. The second. The third falls.*

*She closes her fist. The light folds away. The children stare at her back like it is a wall.*

**ROSE (to the children, calm):**
> "Count to sixty. We are leaving in sixty."

---

### SCENE 4 — THE CALL (Aiko, 60 seconds)

*Aiko's lab. The star-shard fragment pulses. The holographic map shows six points of light —
and every one of them is sending a thin signal line to the same place: the tower.*

*She knows what that means. The shards are calling each other. And something at the tower is answering.*

*She picks up the shard. Blue light runs up her wrist.*

**AIKO (whisper):**
> "…I'm sorry. I have to know."

---

### SCENE 5 — THE MEMORY (Lena, 70 seconds)

*Evidence room. Lena opens the classified locker. Inside: a dead grey shard.*

*She touches it. Blue light. A vision —*

*— the sky cracking, 40 years ago. A boy watching the blue fire fall. A red mark under his eye.*

*— the vision ends. Lena is on her knees. She did not choose this memory.*

**LENA (whisper):**
> "…You were there. You were just a kid. And you've been planning this ever since."

*She puts the shard in her pocket. She is going to need it.*

---

### SCENE 6 — THE POLITICIAN (Vane, 40 seconds)

*Vane shakes hands with the police commissioner. Polite. Warm. Helpful.*

**VANE:**
> "These six are dangerous. They broke the sky. Find them before I have to."
> "My foundation has already donated the drones. Use them."

*He leaves. In the elevator, he adjusts his cuff — and for one second, the glove is translucent.
A red glow inside. A shard, stitched into the leather.*

---

### SCENE 7 — THE STANDOFF (all, 80 seconds)

*The hideout. Aarav and Bo turn — LENA is there, badge up.*

**LENA:**
> "You're all over the news. Congratulations."

**AARAV:**
> "You here to arrest us?"

**LENA:**
> "No. I'm here because a dead thing in a police basement just showed me a forty-year-old boy with a red mark under his eye."
> "And I think you know who that is."

*A shadow moves. GHOST steps out of the wall.*

**GHOST (two voices at once):**
> "The tower is the heart. Cut it, and the shards sleep."
> "He is not your enemy. He is your door."

---

### SCENE 8 — THE RUN (no dialogue, 30 seconds)

*They run through the old city. Drones above. Searchlights. Wet neon. A boy, a nun, a detective,
a scientist's signal already moving through the city's wires.*

*Aiko presses her palm to the window. The blue line of light runs into the building.*

**AIKO (whisper):**
> "Come and find me. I left the back door open."

**— END OF PART 02 —**
