# EVERFALL — PART 04: SHADOW DETECTIVE

> Part 01 `The Crack` · Part 02 `Wanted Sparks` · Part 03 `The Iron Boy` · **Part 04 `Shadow Detective`** · next: Part 05 `Six Bruises`
>
> Logline: Detective Lena Kovac follows the dead shard into the one place no case file can reach — the shadows themselves — and finds her sister, the first Crack, and proof that Malachi Vane has been walking this road for forty years.

## THE RULES OF THIS PART
- **Shadows remember.** Every place a shard crossed, its shadow keeps a looping echo of that day. Shadow-jumpers fall into them by accident.
- **Echo shadows** are the same place, forty years ago. Same crows. Same trams. Same rain.
- **Lena's price is the clock.** Every jump costs her one minute of memory. By this part, the missing minutes have become hours — and she keeps two notebooks so she can lie to herself about it.
- **Vane never raises his voice.** He answers hard questions like a librarian.

## CAST IN THIS PART
LENA (31) — detective, shadow-jumper, losing time.
MARA (9, unchanged for forty years) — Lena's sister, lost in the first Crack.
VANE (62) — soft-spoken, endlessly patient, has been planning this since he was 22.
GHOST — the only one who understands the echoes, because they live in one.
AARAV — just escaped Vane's tower (end of Part 03). Cold, angry, missing his father's face.
AIKO — turned on Vane to save Aarav. Carries the guilt like ballast.
BO — 13, loud, the only one who makes Lena laugh.
ROSE — the nun. Counts the cost of every fight.

## SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — INT. POLICE EVIDENCE LOCKER — NIGHT
Rain on the skylight. Lena sits alone with the dead shard from Part 02 — tagged CASE 1986/4419, *never opened, never closed*. When her shadow slides across it, the shard ticks once. Like a bulb warming up.

> **LENA (V.O.)** — *Every shadow I've ever crossed in this city… I can jump through. But lately something has been jumping back.*

She touches the shard. The locker lights die. Her own shadow crawls up the wall wrong — a second shadow, smaller, holding her hand.

### SCENE 2 — INT. EVIDENCE ROOM — CONTINUOUS
Lena breaks the 40-year seal on BOX 1986. Inside: photographs of the first Crack — the day the sky split the first time — and a child's shoe. Dust rises like it's been waiting.

> **LENA** — *Case 1986/4419. "Girl, age nine, separated from guardian. Status: unresolved."*

The photo shows a runaway-crack in the air and a girl in a yellow coat, reaching INTO the light. The girl's face is Mara's.

### SCENE 3 — THE FIRST ECHO JUMP
Lena steps into her own shadow and comes out in OLD EVERFALL — forty years ago. The same street. Older trams, crows like a dark river, but the CRACK is virgin-bright and small. Then it refracts — and the sky tears.

> **YOUNG VANE (22, off-screen)** — *Dad? Dad — the light. Dad… dad's gone into the light.*

She turns: a young man with gray-blue eyes holds empty gloves — and his father is dissolving upward into the Crack like ink pulled off paper. It is Vane. It was always Vane.

### SCENE 4 — INT. SAFEHOUSE — LATER
Aarav (patched up from the tower), Aiko, Bo, Rose. Lena slaps the box down.

> **AARAV** — *He keeps faces the way you keep case files. Mine is the newest.*
> **AIKO** — *Then your sister —*
> **LENA** — *Don't say "filed."*

Ghost appears mid-sentence, the way they do.

> **GHOST** — *The girl in the yellow coat did not die. Nothing crosses a shard and dies. It is kept. You keep memories. He keeps faces. The sky keeps… people.*

### SCENE 5 — THE TRAM STATION ECHO
Lena jumps again — deliberate now. Rain, a looping tram arriving every sixty seconds, and Mara — nine years old, dry, unaged, reading a worn comic on the platform. She has done this lap a thousand times.

> **MARA** — *You're late. Again.*
> **LENA** — *Mara. I know you don't —*
> **MARA** — *One minute per jump. That's what they take. You're up to hours now, Lee. I can always tell. You smell like torn paper.*

Lena laughs and cries in the same breath.

### SCENE 6 — THE TOWER'S LONG SHADOW
Lena follows the tower's shadow at noon and falls into the oldest echo in the city: the night of the first Crack itself. 22-year-old Vane kneels in glass rain, making a promise to a sky that isn't listening.

> **YOUNG VANE** — *If you took him into the light, then I will reach into the light and take something back. Every year. Whatever it costs. Whatever it eats.*

Forty years of patience, born in one night. Then — *snap* — the echo notices her. The whole scene turns to face her at once. She jumps out.

### SCENE 7 — INT. FOGGED PIER — NIGHT (CONFRONTATION)
Lena puts a photo of Mara on the wet wood between them. Vane looks at it the way a man looks at an old library card.

> **LENA** — *You knew her. You've known about her for forty years.*
> **VANE** — *I know what the sky keeps, Detective. Your sister is not dead. She is stored. Between stamps, in the dark, dry and nine. I could show you where.*
> **LENA** — *Price.*
> **VANE** — *The key — your iron boy — finishes what I started. Then the light opens all the way. And everything it ever kept… comes back out.*

Lena pockets the photo. She has heard enough to be dangerous.

### SCENE 8 — EXT. SAFEHOUSE ROOF — DAWN
Lena burns her "official" notebook — the one full of missing-time lies — in a coffee can. Blue fire, shard-fed. She keeps the private one: two notebooks, one truth.

> **LENA (V.O.)** — *Forty years ago the sky took my sister and made a monster with a receipt for everything. Fine. Then we open the sky on OUR terms — and audit the light.*

Aarav watches the blue fire. Neither of them sleeps.

> **AARAV** — *My father's face. Your sister. Bo's hearing. Rose's years. He keeps receipts for all of it.*
> **LENA** — *Then we rob him.*

FADE OUT — Part 05: **SIX BRUISES**.
